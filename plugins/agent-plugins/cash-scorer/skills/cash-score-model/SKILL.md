# Cash Score Model

## Philosophy

Money is the only game every company on the planet is playing. The scorecard is Cash.

The Cash Score is a 0–100 composite rating of cash quality across five dimensions: yield, conversion, efficiency, stability, and deployment.

| Dimension | Max Points |
|---|---|
| Cash Yield | 20 |
| Cash Conversion | 20 |
| Cash Efficiency | 20 |
| Cash Stability | 20 |
| Cash Deployment | 20 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Score interpretation: 80–100 = Elite, 65–79 = Strong, 50–64 = Average, 35–49 = Weak, 0–34 = Poor
